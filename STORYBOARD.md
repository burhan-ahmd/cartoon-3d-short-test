# Shot plan — The Moon That Got Away

30 fps; 300 frames; one continuous camera move, no frozen holds.

| Frames | Beat | Lens / camera | Character action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-59 | Balloon slips free | Low wide establishes lantern market; push and tilt to rising balloon | Child reaches; seller turns; baker steps out carrying tray |
| 60-149 | The chase | Crane forward between foreground stall and flower bicycle; arc right | Child runs, seller hurries while holding apron, baker jogs with wobbling toque |
| 150-239 | Near escape | Rise with balloon then whip-pan down toward ribbon | Seller catches the string; child grabs beneath; baker brakes and windmills arms |
| 240-299 | Bonk and button | Dolly into trio, slight dutch kick on impact, settle while still creeping | Balloon bounces down onto baker's hat; all three react and laugh |

Everything is deterministic from absolute frame time. Camera and poses have explicit continuous functions; no randomness.
